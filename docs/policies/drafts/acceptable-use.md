## 1. Scope

requests through Chat Completions, and to any use of the catalog, model prices,

## 2. Prohibited uses

You must not use the service to:

1. Break the law in [JURISDICTION] or in a jurisdiction where you or the
   upstream provider operate.
2. Generate or distribute child sexual abuse material, or content that
   sexually exploits a minor.
3. Threaten, harass, or incite violence against a person or group.
4. Create or distribute malware, run denial-of-service attacks, or attempt to
   gain unauthorized access to any system.
5. Circumvent this service's limits: rotate keys to exceed quotas, evade rate

6. Resell or provide access to the gateway to third parties without the

7. Infringe intellectual property, or violate another person's privacy by

## 3. Prompt and output responsibility

You are responsible for the content you submit and for how you use the output.
The gateway does not screen prompts or outputs for policy violations and does
not store their content. Upstream providers may apply their own policies and may
refuse or filter a request; the gateway reports that refusal but cannot override
it.

## 4. Enforcement

The operator may, after review:

- refuse or rate-limit requests;
- suspend a key or an account;
- freeze credit while a dispute or abuse review is open;
- terminate the account for a serious or repeated breach.

Where practical, the operator will record the reason on a support request. A
suspension for suspected fraud, a legal demand, or an imminent risk may happen
before notice.

## 5. Reporting abuse

Report abuse or a security issue through the account support queue with the
`support` or `privacy` type. Include the model id, timestamps and what you
observed; do not include another person's credentials or personal data.
