# Savant ecosystem — model quality and price

The operator's brand direction is cyberpunk/neon noir. Cyan is the primary
logo color; neon accents are intentional identity, not something to remove.

## Direction

The visual authority is the operator's core product reference:
`C:/Users/spenc/dev/savant-code/assets/landing.html` (read-only).
Use its electric cyan/magenta synthwave language, Orbitron display lettering,
Rajdhani body typography, JetBrains Mono technical labels, HUD corners,
outlined headings, atmospheric city/horizon/grid layers, and selective glow.
The reference supplies visual identity; its coding-product claims do not
transfer to Gateway. A screenshot is the selected external design reference.
Build recognizable compositions from real model catalogs, request payloads,
usage, and account controls. No fabricated uptime, customers, throughput,
latency, certifications, trial availability, or payment readiness.

Palette from Savant Code: ink #030408; panel #070b14; raised #0b1120;
cyan #00fbff; magenta #ff2a6d; purple #b537f2; yellow #fcee0a;
green #00ff9f; text #c9d4e8; original dim #66748f.
Raise dim text contrast where needed rather than blindly reproducing faint text.
Cyan primary actions use dark text. Glow belongs on one or two focal edges,
not on body text. Maintain readable 4.5:1 body-text contrast.

Typography: Orbitron 500/700/900 display, Rajdhani 400–700 body, JetBrains Mono
400/500/700 for ids, prices, code, and quiet metadata. Avoid paragraphs in all caps.
Use 4–8px corners on utility surfaces, straight table rows, asymmetric landing
composition, and clear sections. No endless equally weighted rounded cards.
Public pages can be expressive; account and admin must remain efficient.

Use real Savant logo assets. Never replace with initials or invented marks.
Use Font Awesome Free icons and HeroUI v3 components in implementation.
Honor focus visibility, keyboard controls, touch targets, reduced motion,
mobile navigation, and overflow handling. Decoration never masks data.

## Whole-site application

- Landing: recognizable SAVANT // GATEWAY ecosystem identity, distinctive hero,
  prominent model pricing and useful price comparison, visible API interaction.
- Catalog and docs: precise typography, clear filters and pricing hierarchy,
  practical reading widths and code panels.
- Authentication: identity-led composition with direct, accessible forms.
- Account: wallet, keys, usage, and history first; factual setup states.
- Admin and console: dense operational workspace, grouped navigation that
  remains reachable on mobile, shared tables/forms/status colors.
- Errors, empty states, loading and confirmation screens follow the same system.

The trial-to-paid proposal is not implemented. Keep pre-launch limitations
honest; do not render a fictitious active trial balance or successful checkout.

## Positioning correction — 2026-10-29

Operator: "the whole 'build after dark' is the wrong direction, we need to focus
on price quality". Remove that tagline and the after-hours positioning entirely.
The palette describes the brand; the product promise concerns model access,
quality, price transparency, and value.

Suggested headline: "Leading models. Clearer costs." Explain that customers
compare model input/output prices, choose what suits the workload, and pay
through prepaid credit. Do not claim a percentage saving, identical output
quality to direct vendors, guaranteed availability, or benchmark leadership
without evidence. Do not turn decorative telemetry into fictional statistics.
Use sample catalog figures only with a visible illustrative-data label.
